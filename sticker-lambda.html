The Lambda

Identitarian 2 October 2026

A round sticker with no words at all: just a black lambda inside a black ring on a yellow background. That is exactly the point: it is meant to be recognised by those who already know it.

What it shows

The Greek letter lambda (Λ) inside a circle, in yellow and black. The design is minimal and easy to reproduce, so it works as a logo, a badge or a quick tag.

Who uses it

The lambda in a circle is the emblem of the identitarian movement. It was adopted by the French group Génération Identitaire, which was dissolved by the French government in March 2021, and by sister groups in other European countries, including Italy.

The symbol refers to the shields of the Spartans at Thermopylae, as popularised by the film 300: the identitarians present themselves as a small elite "defending" Europe from immigration.

How to recognise it

Look for the yellow-and-black colour pair and the lambda shape. Because the sticker carries no text or web address, it is often overlooked or mistaken for a brand logo.
