Defend Evropa

Nationalist 11 October 2026

A black-and-white sticker with a charging crusader knight, the slogan "Defend Evropa" and the logo and website of La Dissidence Française.

What it shows

A mounted knight with a cross on his chest charges with a raised sword. Below, a circular arrow logo sits next to the words "DEFEND EVROPA". A red band carries the web address of La Dissidence Française and its hashtag.

What it means

Crusader imagery is used across the European far right to frame Europe as a Christian fortress under attack, a message aimed mainly at Muslims and migrants.

The spelling "Evropa", with a V in place of the U, imitates Latin inscriptions. It is a common device to suggest an ancient, "eternal" European identity.

Who is behind it

La Dissidence Française is a French nationalist movement founded in 2011. The sticker is clearly signed: it carries the movement's name, website and social media icons.
