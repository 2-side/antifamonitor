GUD Rat

Student groups 16 October 2026

A black sticker with a cartoon rat in sunglasses, the slogan "Europe, Jeunesse, Révolution!" and the name and website of the Groupe Union Défense.

What it shows

A rat wearing a beret and sunglasses points at the viewer. Below it, hand-lettered text reads "Europe, Jeunesse, Révolution!". At the bottom is a Celtic cross, the words "GROUPE UNION DEFENSE" and a red band with the group's web address.

Who is behind it

The GUD (Groupe Union Défense) is a French far-right student group founded in 1968 at the Assas law faculty in Paris. It has a long record of street violence and was dissolved by the French government in 2024.

The black rat is the group's historic mascot. The Celtic cross is a white-supremacist symbol widely used by neo-fascist groups.

How to recognise it

The rat drawing is the giveaway, often paired with a Celtic cross and the colours black, white and red. The cartoon style is meant to make the message look playful and attract young people.
