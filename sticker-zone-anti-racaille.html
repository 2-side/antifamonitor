Zone Anti-Racaille

Local groups 6 October 2026

A rectangular blue sticker declaring the area a "Zone Anti-Racaille", with a dark silhouette holding a bat in front of a city skyline and a web address at the bottom.

What it shows

Large white letters read "ZONE ANTI-RACAILLE". Below, a black silhouette shoulders a baseball bat over a skyline whose towers recall Lyon's Fourvière basilica. A web address referring to Lyon runs along the bottom edge.

What it means

"Racaille" ("scum") is a derogatory word that in French political language is aimed above all at young people from working-class and immigrant neighbourhoods.

Combined with the bat, the message is a vigilante one: it marks territory and suggests that certain people are not welcome there, under threat of violence.

Attribution

The group behind this sticker has not been verified yet. The local skyline and the web address point to the Lyon area, where several far-right groups have been active. If you have more information, get in touch.
